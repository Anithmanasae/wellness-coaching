District General Hospital Kilinochchi · Orthopaedic Surgery Ward 16

What to Expect on Surgery Day

Shot list for Google Flow (Veo), in Sri Lankan Tamil. There are 15 clips of 8 seconds each, about 2 minutes in total. Vikash plays the doctor, and every speaker talks in Sri Lankan Tamil. Make each clip, save it with the file name shown, then send all the clips back to be joined. The hospital name, ward title and Tamil/English subtitles are added afterwards.

Before you start

Photo A: white coatVikash in a white coat with a stethoscope. Used in clips 1, 2, 13 and 14.
Photo B: theatre scrubsVikash in green scrubs and a blue cap, with his mask lowered. Used in clips 6 and 8.
  1. Open labs.google/flow and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Tap New project.
  3. For clips marked Vikash, choose Ingredients to Video and upload Photo A or Photo B. For all other clips, choose Text to Video.
  4. Set the video to landscape 16:9 with sound on.
  5. Tap Copy prompt below, paste it into Flow and generate.
  6. Download the clip and rename it to the file name shown, for example clip00.mp4.

About the Tamil speech: Veo's Tamil can sound slightly unnatural, and lips may not match perfectly. If a line sounds wrong, mispronounced or cut off, generate it again. The Tamil lines were written by AI, so ask a Tamil-speaking colleague to read them before the video is shown to patients.

About signs: AI video can't write the hospital name correctly on buildings or signs. The prompts ask for no readable text, and the name "District General Hospital Kilinochchi · Orthopaedic Surgery Ward 16" is added as a clean title afterwards.

The 15 clips

Check each clip before keeping it

  • Vikash's face looks like him.
  • The Tamil line is said fully and clearly, with nothing cut off or added.
  • No garbled signs or writing in the background.
  • No needles going into skin, and nothing frightening for patients.
  • Gloves, wristbands and monitors look right.

When you're done

Send all 15 files (clip00.mp4 to clip14.mp4) back in one message. They will be joined in order, with the hospital title, Tamil and English subtitles, and an end card added.
